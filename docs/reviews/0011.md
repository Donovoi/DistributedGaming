Date: 2026-11-24. Scope: static resource and cost mapping. Progress stays at 50%. M5 remains pending.

## Round 1: design challenge

The design reviewer advised against another full offline transport demo before finding the live boundary. The selected reduction has no benefit margin under the recorded configuration and proposed per-job transfer design. The reviewer required separate treatment of sampled event time, serialization bounds, and total critical-path cost.

The reviewer accepted this map's limited claims. Recorded extents do not prove non-aliasing across lifetimes or device-address accesses. The output copy identifies a destination, not its eventual consumer. The semaphore's color-output wait does not cover every compute/copy operation. The highest sampled event is not a bound on whole game work.

The next step remains observation only. The copy destination's owner, mapping lifetime, completion rule, and access interval must be known before live read/writeback. API-only observation cannot reveal ordinary CPU loads through a persistent mapping.

It still found zero recorded overlap candidates.
